Usage · Notifications

Know when it's done.

Long tasks finish while you're elsewhere. DayMug can tell you in the browser, or push to your phone through Bark or PushDeer.

In the browser

Allow notifications when your browser asks. DayMug then shows Task completed, Task failed or Waiting for your input for a conversation you aren't looking at — nothing for the one already on screen. Clicking it opens the conversation. With several DayMug tabs open you get one notification, not one per tab.

On your phone

  1. Install an app — Bark (iOS) or PushDeer.
  2. Paste its key under Settings → Notifications: the Bark URL (https://api.day.app/your-key) or the PushDeer key (PDU…). With both set, Notification channel picks one.
  3. Save. Every conversation notifies by default.

To quiet one conversation, silence it; the conversation list then shows a crossed-out bell next to it — tap the bell to turn notifications back on. Scheduled tasks have their own Send notification switch (Scheduled tasks).

Make taps open the conversation

Set server.public_url in config.yaml to the address people use, so a tap on the push opens the right page.
